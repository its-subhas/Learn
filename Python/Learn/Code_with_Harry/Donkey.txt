A Donkeys is a hardworking and intelligent domestic animal. 
It is known for its strength, patience, and ability to carry heavy loads over long distances.
 Donkeys are commonly found in villages and are often used by farmers and laborers to transport goods.
  They have long ears, a short mane, and a strong body. 
  Although people sometimes use the word "donkey" to describe someone as stubborn, donkeys are actually cautious animals that avoid dangerous situations. 
  They are friendly, loyal, and can live for many years when properly cared for. 
  Donkeys play an important role in agriculture and transportation in many parts of the world.
